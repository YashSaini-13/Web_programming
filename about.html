About PitchWire

PitchWire is a site built for one purpose: keeping up with soccer shouldn't mean checking five different apps just to find out when your team plays next.

I got the idea for pitchwire because I was tired of the scramble every weekend, one app for scores, another for transfer news, a third tab open just to check kickoff times across time zones. Soccer is a global sport with matches happening at all hours across dozens of leagues, and I wanted a single place where I could see the news that mattered and the schedule I actually cared about, without digging through noise about leagues I don't follow.

What really pushed me to start building was missing a match I'd been looking forward to all week, simply because the kickoff time got moved and I found out an hour too late. That kind of thing happens constantly when your information is scattered. PitchWire pulls schedules, results, and headlines together so that never happens again and it's grown from a personal fix into something I hope other fans find just as useful.

Why I keep building it

I've realized how many fans run into the same problem where there are too many sources, not enough signal. Every feature I plan now starts from the same question: does this help someone catch kickoff on time and understand what's actually happening in the sport, without wading through clutter to get there?

What's on PitchWire

Right now, PitchWire covers match schedules and results across major leagues, breaking transfer and club news, and short daily roundups for fans who just want the highlights.
